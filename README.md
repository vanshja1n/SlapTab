## ⚡ The Problem

Distractions don't ask for permission. You open a tab *"just for a second"* to check Instagram, X, or YouTube—and two hours disappear.

**SlapTab** takes back control of your focus. When your daily active time on a distracting site runs out, SlapTab blocks the page immediately and forces you to solve a quick math quiz to earn access back.

---

## ✨ Features

- **⏱️ Active Time Tracking:** Set custom daily time limits per domain (e.g., 15 mins on X, 30 mins on YouTube).
- **💥 Instant Slap (Block Screen):** Triggers a clean frosted-glass overlay the second your limit is up.
- **🧠 Math Quiz Unlock:** Forces cognitive friction—solve a quick math problem to gain temporary access back.
- **⚙️ Custom Unlock Duration:** Choose how many minutes of grace time you get after solving a quiz before getting slapped again.
- **🔒 Offline First:** Operates 100% locally on your browser. Fast, light, and private.
